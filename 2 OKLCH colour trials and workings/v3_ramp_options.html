Wallace v3 — green ramp options

Four complete systems, ordered left to right by background lightness. Each panel is drawn on the background that option would actually create, with its own foreground green and equal-lightness accent row. Every accent shown passes its bar against that background (worst hue is always magenta). The foreground green remains the green of the accent row in all options.

  • Equal temperament was tried and rejected: a constant contrast ratio per step (geometric in luminance, anchored on the base green) needs ratio 1.504 once the worst hue is satisfied, which pushes the background darker (#001000, L 0.140). Compression that lightens the background has to come from the step grid instead, which is what A, B and C do.
  • B keeps a uniform ramp and simply refines it: 5 steps a side, background = dark4. Its AA accents land at 5.2:1 minimum — extra headroom.
  • C keeps the familiar light1/light2 foreground rows but its stretched light side pushes light4 (the cursor) past the gamut: it clamps to pure white #ffffff.
  • A is the strongest pull toward base (~0.83× step): background #002909 sits about halfway between the old dark3 and the too-bright dark2 (#00360c).
